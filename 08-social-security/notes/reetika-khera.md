# Revdi ya Haq

## Chapter 3: Anganwadis / ICDS

* p49: beneficiaries are kids younger than six, their mothers and to some
  extent, young girls.
* Anganwadis are the points of contact for ICDS.
* p50: NFHS is invaluable: nutrition, health, domestic violence experiences too,
  as well as beneficiaries of govt schemes.
* NFHS-4 was delayed by nearly a decade.
* p51: As percent of GDP, ICDS spend in 2004-5 was 0.05% to 0.15% in 2014-15,
  and from then to 2023-24 it's down to 0.07%.
* p52: Bihar, Gujarat and Odisha - see what's with them: very templates of good
  and bad implementation practices. They show how proactivity of the government
  can actually remove the most obstinate barriers.
* p54: For every 1000 people there's supposed to be one Anganwadi; which
  provides the following six services:
    - nutrition
    - preschooling
    - vaccination
    - growth monitoring
    - health referral
* p55: broadly, in the first decade (2004-14) was about laying the foundations,
  and the next decade was about quality.
* p59: why Bangladesh might be a good model / comparison.
* p61: Normally, as GDP grows, countries usually spend _more_ on social
  security.
* After the 14th finance commission, the responsibilities of states grew, but
  their fund sources dried up; by GST and various cesses.
* Even Anganwardi workers have occasionally striked. In TN, Anganwadi workers
  are treated as government employees, and get the corresponding benefits like
  pension, maternity leave, etc. Some workers have had to spend out of their own
  pockets.
* p62: March 2018 - Poshan abhiyaan.
* ICDS-CAS: Common application software - unclear whether it hurts or helps.
* p63: Gujarat CM calls the helpline and it goes unanswered; nobody tries to find out
  why. It was spun as workers being lazy.
* The CAS likely does more harm than good because a single centralized software
  is perhaps not a good idea. Various states and regions have their own hours of
  operation and other processes.
* p64: Corporate lobbying (hiding under the guise of CSR). What was once a conflict
  of interest is now a win-win situation.
* Money spent on bullshit stuff like smartphones, software and large meetings
  and festivals and conferences supposedly awareness and behaviour change.
* p65: Modi wrote an open letter to the then PM, Manmohan Singh, saying that the
  NFSA was insufficient. Now his own govertment is weakening it even more.
